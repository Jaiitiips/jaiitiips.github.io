A little more clarity

Before your next session.

Who is WorkoutMaker for?

Anyone who wants a clear place to organise workouts and record strength or cardio training. Build a schedule that suits your routine and use your history to keep track of your work.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Your workout information is stored locally in the app on your device.

Can I share my weekly schedule?

Yes. Use the schedule export option in Settings, then choose where to save or share the file.

How can I get help or suggest a feature?

Email Jaiitriips@gmail.com. For a technical issue, include your app version, iOS version and what happened. Please avoid sending private workout information unless you want to share it.
