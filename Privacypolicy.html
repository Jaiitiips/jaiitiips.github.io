Information stored on your device

WorkoutMaker stores your workout plans, current workout results, saved sessions, exercise history, custom movements and preferences locally on your device. This information allows the app to record workouts, display progress and remember your settings. You do not need an account to use the app.

The app does not send your workout information to us. It does not include advertising, third-party analytics or tracking cookies, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Backups, imports and sharing

You can choose to export a schedule or a full backup. A full backup includes your workout data, history, custom movements and preferences. Exports may contain information you consider private. You decide where to save them and who to share them with.

If you save or share a file through iCloud Drive or another service, that service handles the file under its own privacy policy. WorkoutMaker does not give us access to those files. Device backups managed by Apple may also contain app data, depending on your device settings.

Importing a schedule or restoring a backup reads a file you select. Restoring a full backup replaces the app's existing data after confirmation.

Notifications

With your permission, WorkoutMaker uses local notifications to notify you when a workout timer finishes. You can change notification permission in your device's Settings.

Managing your data

You can correct or delete saved sessions and use the reset options in Settings to clear progress history or the weekly schedule. Those reset options have different scopes and do not each erase every category of app data. Deleting the app removes its local app data; separately saved exports, shared copies and device backups must be managed separately through the relevant service.

Support and external links

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to include in your message and use it to respond. Do not include private workout data unless you want to share it for support.

Opening the privacy-policy website takes you outside the app. The website hosting provider may process ordinary web-request information, such as an IP address, under its own policy.
